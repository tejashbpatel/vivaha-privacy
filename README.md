# Vivaha – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Vivaha helps you plan a wedding in one place: your budget, your options and costs for each part of the event, your vendors and your guest list.

## The short version

- Everything you enter stays on your phone.
- We don't collect, see or receive your data.
- Vivaha has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.

## Who we are

Vivaha ("Vivaha", "the app") is made by Tejash Patel ("we", "us"). You can reach us at tejashbpatel@gmail.com.

## What you enter in the app

You may enter things like your budget, the options and costs you are comparing for each category, vendor names and contact details, guest names and any details you choose to add, and notes. This information is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

## What we collect

Nothing. We have no servers that receive your information, and the app does not connect to any service that gives us access to what you enter.

- No account or sign-in is needed.
- The app does not read your contacts.
- No advertising identifiers, analytics, or third-party tracking tools.

## Sharing with another family

Vivaha can export your wedding plan as a file. You choose how to send it (for example AirDrop, WhatsApp or Mail), and it only goes where you send it. If someone imports the file, they get their own independent copy, not a live connection back to you. Apart from that, Vivaha does not send your data to anyone.

## Backups

Your phone's own backups (for example iCloud Backup or a computer backup) may include the app's data. That is handled by Apple under your own device settings, not by us.

## Deleting your data

You can delete any entry inside the app. Deleting the app from your phone removes all of its data from that device.

## Crash reports

If you've chosen to share app analytics with developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports. We use them only to fix bugs.

## Children

Vivaha is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Future features

If we add features that change how your information is handled (for example live sharing with family, iCloud sync or online invitations), we will update this policy before they are released and tell you in the app.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
